  frequency_evidence.json.

STABILITY GATE (fixed now; will NOT be adjusted after seeing results)
  Gate statistic (primary):
      spread_pct = (max - min) / max * 100
  over ALL exec-window observed_pct_perf_cpu0 values of the controlled run.
  This is exactly the frozen G7 pairwise formula
  abs(x-y)/max(abs(x),abs(y))*100 evaluated on the extreme pair (min, max).
  Gate: spread_pct <= 5.0  =>  PASS.

  Also reported (secondary, non-gating, reported for transparency):
      spread_over_mean_pct = (max - min) / mean * 100
      spread_over_min_pct  = (max - min) / min * 100
      CV = sample_stdev / mean * 100   (n-1 denominator)
      min, max, mean, median

IN-EXECUTION RAMP (fixed now)
  An exec shows a MATERIAL in-execution frequency/timing ramp if EITHER:
    (a) |last10_median_us - first10_median_us| / first10_median_us * 100 > 5.0
        (within-window timing drift over the 50 measured samples), OR
    (b) |observed_pct_perf_after - observed_pct_perf_before|
        / observed_pct_perf_before * 100 > 5.0
        (bracketing PDH %perf change across the exec).
  Timeline samples (100 ms) that fall inside an exec window are reported as
  additional evidence where coverage exists.

POST-GAP VS WARM (reported, not gating by itself)
  Mean exec-window %perf for after_idle_gap=true vs after_idle_gap=false.

DECISION RULES (fixed now)
  C - CONTROL FAILED : apply failed, or restore failed / original scheme not
                       proven restored after the session.
  B - CONTROL INSUFFICIENT : apply+restore succeeded but spread_pct > 5.0
                       or a material in-execution ramp was observed.
  A - CONTROL VALIDATED : apply+restore succeeded AND spread_pct <= 5.0 AND
                       no material in-execution ramp.
