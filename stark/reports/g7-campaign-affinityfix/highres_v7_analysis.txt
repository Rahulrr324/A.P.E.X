7. STEP DETERMINATION (requirement 8: A / B / C / D)
   A. Frequency GENUINELY VARIES during benchmark execution:
      SUPPORTED and dominant. 6/8 covered B execs show within-exec
      % Processor Performance spreads of 9.1..72.3% -- several EXCEED the
      6.8974% gate value inside a single exec. Shapes: exec-start boost
      ramps (exec7: 123.6 -> 205.4 within one ~7 ms exec), mid-exec dips
      (exec1: min 60.6), sustained I/O-bursty modulation (exec11/12:
      78..150 transitions over 116..235 ms).
   B. The previous 6.8974% was primarily between-exec/sampling artifact:
      NOT SUPPORTED as the primary explanation: variation lives INSIDE
      execs and often exceeds 6.8974% there. The 2 B-side execs (<=1%
      in-exec spread) show the counter also holds steady during
      back-to-back execs already at operating frequency -- so variation
      is state-dependent (idle transitions, exec starts, I/O burstiness),
      not constant counter noise.
   C. The collector itself perturbs execution:
      TRUE for the v5/v6 architectures (B7 +59..100% paired; external probe:
      tight-loop collection = -25% worker throughput) -- documented and
      now FIXED. For the v7 mechanism: B7 and B9 are within the predeclared
      2.0% (-1.96%, +1.79%), B6 pooled is material in the FASTER direction
      (-5.71%) with inconsistent pair signs and cold-start/order
      confounding -> NOT clean, see limitation L1.
   D. Indeterminate: NOT applicable. Coverage 8/8, PDH adequate, pins
      verified on both threads -- the data is decidable.
   => OVERALL DETERMINATION FOR THIS STEP: **A -- genuine within-execution
      frequency variation**, with the qualifications in L1 (B6 timing
      materiality) and L2 (B9 margin at the floor).

       what "load" does to this counter's basis on this hybrid CPU; P1/P2
       are used ONLY for response-time adequacy (both pass by orders of
       magnitude), never as absolute frequency truth.

       restored and GUID-verified. The machine is a shared laptop (Chrome/
       OneDrive/Zed present); background load cannot be excluded as a
       contributor to exec-level timing noise (~5% band observed within a
