7. L11-G -- Comp(A2) - Comp(A9) >= -0.10
==============================================================================
A2 and A9 are matched pairs: identical evaluation seed and identical
streams; only training order differs.  The 360 evaluation cues are the
same objects, so the two proportions are PAIRED and the correct test is
McNemar.  var(d) = (psi - delta^2)/n, psi = P(discordant).
     p    psi  sd paired  P(fail|delta=0)  sd unpaired  P(fail|delta=0)
  0.90   0.02     0.0129         4.72e-15       0.0387         4.91e-03
  0.90   0.05     0.0204         4.82e-07       0.0387         4.91e-03
  0.90   0.10     0.0289         2.66e-04       0.0387         4.91e-03
  0.90   0.20     0.0408         7.15e-03       0.0387         4.91e-03
  0.95   0.02     0.0129         4.72e-15       0.0281         1.90e-04
  0.95   0.05     0.0204         4.82e-07       0.0281         1.90e-04
  0.95   0.10     0.0289         2.66e-04       0.0281         1.90e-04
  0.95   0.20     0.0408         7.15e-03       0.0281         1.90e-04

the criterion is one-sided with a 0.10 slack: at delta = 0 it fails with
probability < 1e-4 for plausible discordance, i.e. it is nearly non-binding;
at delta = -0.10 exactly it passes with probability 0.5.
three seeds, no multiplicity control: family error <= 3 x the per-seed rate.
